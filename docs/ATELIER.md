# Nacre Atelier

The living explorer for Shader Grammar v0.1.

It is not a shader pack and not a schema browser. It is a lookdev studio
that drives one WebGL sketch from bound operators, fields, and parameters.

## What it is for

- **Look** — twelve specimens as optical objects. Fingerprint, artistic macros
  (Pearl, Liquidity, Restlessness, Depth), and clickable operators that light
  every other specimen sharing that primitive.
- **Fuse** — mix two recipes by operator weight. Conservative when the family
  is shared (oil × soap), speculative when it is not (nacre × nebula).
- **Proof** — the coverage matrix and the validation gates. Generalization is
  visible, not asserted.

## What it must not do

- Name a material as an operator.
- Ship a unique shader per specimen.
- Treat the canvas as evidence that Airy or Mie are implemented.

The grammar package remains the source of truth. This atelier consumes
`bundle.json` produced by `tools/validate.mjs`.
